Explore the DigComp 3.0 Framework

DigComp 3.0 describes the knowledge, skills and attitudes people need to use digital technologies confidently, critically and responsibly.

The framework contains five competence areas and 21 competences. Select an area to understand what it covers, how it relates to Queensland Government work and the practical capabilities employees can develop.
