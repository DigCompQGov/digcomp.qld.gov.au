Queensland Government digital capability

Proof of concept

The CDSB Digital Capability Team is testing an end-to-end approach to assess Queensland public sector digital capability and connect development needs with practical learning pathways.

A cross-agency cohort is expected to participate in late 2026. Findings may inform future decisions about a scalable digital capability solution. No future solution or investment has been determined.

Find out more about the proof of concept ▸

About the proof of concept

The Department of Customer Services, Open Data and Small and Family Business is exploring how assessment, capability baselining and targeted learning pathways could support digital capability uplift across Queensland Government.

CDSB has developed an assessment tool to help participating Queensland Government employees understand their current capabilities against DigComp 3.0.

The proof of concept will test whether this approach can identify capability strengths and development needs, support applied learning and provide useful evidence for workforce planning.

Participation will be coordinated by the Digital Capability Team. The work is exploratory and does not commit Queensland Government to a future platform, service or investment.

Interested in getting involved?

Queensland Government employees and agencies interested in participating in the proof of concept, contributing feedback or learning more about the proposed approach can contact the Digital Capability Team.

THE QUEENSLAND DIGITAL CAPABILITY FRAMEWORK

The framework behind Queensland's digital capability approach

DigComp 3.0 is the foundation of the Queensland Government Digital Capability Framework. It provides a shared, evidence-based way to understand, assess and develop digital capability across the public sector.

The Australian Government Department of Employment and Workplace Relations (DEWR) recognises DigComp as a preferred framework for digital skills and workforce development across industries and occupations.

DigComp helps organisations and individuals to:

  • understand digital capability
  • identify strengths and development needs
  • design learning and development pathways
  • support workplace capability uplift
  • track digital capability growth over time

About DigComp 3.0

DigComp 3.0 is the latest version of the European Commission's Digital Competence Framework. It describes the knowledge, skills and attitudes people need to use digital technologies confidently, critically and responsibly in work, learning and everyday life.

The framework contains:

  • 5 capability areas
  • 21 competences
  • multiple proficiency levels

It also incorporates contemporary topics such as artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, digital rights, wellbeing, sustainability and misinformation.

Judgement in digital environments

DigComp recognises that digital capability is more than the ability to use technology.

People also need to evaluate information, understand risk, apply critical thinking and make responsible decisions when using digital technologies, including AI.

This emphasis on judgement is embedded throughout the framework.

Why it matters in Queensland

Queensland agencies can use DigComp to support a consistent approach to digital capability development, workforce planning, learning design and capability self-assessment.

Together with complementary frameworks such as SFIA, DigComp helps build a digitally capable workforce that can adapt to changing technologies and deliver better services for Queenslanders.
